### What's new

- **EV charging** — Add your car, picked from a built-in database of ~1,600 EVs and plug-in hybrids (or entered manually). Tap it, enter your current and target charge, and SweetSpot works out how long charging takes and the cheapest window to plug in — based on your car's AC limit and your home charger.
- **"Ready by" time** — Optionally set a deadline so the chosen window finishes by a time you pick. Works for any appliance or charge.
- **Appliance power rating** — Give an appliance its power in kW and the estimated cost now reflects its real consumption instead of a flat 1 kW load.
