### What's new

- **All-in price (Netherlands)** — Optionally see the full consumer price instead of the bare market price. SweetSpot adds energy tax, your supplier's per-kWh surcharge, and VAT on top of the spot price, so the cost cards, breakdown, and chart all show what you'll actually pay. Turn it on under **Total price** in Settings and pick your supplier from the list — or enter your own surcharge. It's display-only: it never changes which window is cheapest, and the chart still shows an honest "you're being paid" state only when the all-in price is truly below zero.

This is the first phase of all-in pricing, currently available for the Netherlands. More countries will follow as tariff data becomes available.
