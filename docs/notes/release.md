### What's new

- **Total price, broken down** — with the total price shown, the upcoming-prices chart now splits every bar into its parts: the market price, energy tax and your supplier's surcharge, with a colour legend. You can see at a glance how little of your bill actually changes through the day.
- **Total / market switch** — a quick toggle on the results screen flips between the estimated total price and the bare market price, without going into Settings.
- **Price tooltip** — press and hold the price chart (and slide) to read the exact price for any moment, down to the market's 15-minute resolution — with the full component breakdown when the total price is on.
- **EV charging time** — the results screen now shows the total charging time in the header, alongside the state-of-charge range.
