### What's new

- **All-in price refinements** — The custom-surcharge field now shows your currency (e.g. `€/kWh`), so the unit is unambiguous. Settings keeps your scroll position when you come back from a picker instead of jumping to the top. And if the total price is switched on but you haven't chosen a supplier or entered a surcharge, SweetSpot now reminds you before you leave Settings.

A small follow-up to the 6.1 all-in price feature (currently available in the Netherlands).
