SweetSpot finds the cheapest time to run your appliances and charge your electric car. Pick how long a device needs to run — or choose your EV and the charge you want — and SweetSpot scans the next 24 hours of dynamic electricity prices to find the cheapest window.

Whether it's a washing machine, dishwasher, dryer, heat pump, or EV charger — start it at the right time and pay less for the same electricity.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose an appliance or your EV, or set a custom duration — and an optional "ready by" deadline
2. SweetSpot fetches the latest day-ahead electricity prices for your region
3. See exactly when to start, with a full cost breakdown per time slot

EV CHARGING
Add your car from a built-in database of around 1,600 EVs and plug-in hybrids — or enter the battery size and charging power yourself. Set your current and target charge, and SweetSpot works out how long charging will take and the cheapest time to plug in.

FEATURES
- 30 European countries, 43 bidding zones — from Portugal to Finland, Norway to Greece
- 5 data sources with automatic fallback (ENTSO-E, Spot-Hinta.fi, Energy-Charts, EnergyZero, aWATTar)
- "Ready by" deadline — only picks a window that finishes in time
- Power ratings — set an appliance's power in kW for a cost estimate that reflects what it really uses
- Save your appliances with custom names, icons, and durations for one-tap access
- Wear OS companion app for Pixel Watch and other Wear OS 3+ devices
- Available in 25 European languages
- No accounts, no analytics, no tracking — prices are cached locally on your device
- Open source (GPL v3) — full source code on GitHub
- Free 14-day trial, then a yearly subscription to keep using the app

SUPPORTED COUNTRIES
Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland.

NOTE
Prices shown are wholesale day-ahead spot prices and do not include VAT, energy tax, or supplier fees. Tomorrow's prices are typically available after 13:00 CET. A dynamic electricity contract is required to benefit from price differences.
