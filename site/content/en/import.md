## Open in the app

This link carries a shared SweetSpot setup — a list of appliances and EV charging settings from someone in your household.

If you have SweetSpot installed, it should open automatically to a preview where you can review and import the setup.

**Nothing happened?** Make sure SweetSpot is installed and up to date, then open the link again from your phone.

## Don't have SweetSpot yet?

[Get SweetSpot on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=today.sweetspot), then reopen the link on your phone.
